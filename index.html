Her story, in her words

I'm writing this because I need help becoming financially independent and eventually leaving my home safely.

I'm a college student, and for a long time my parents have been extremely restrictive about my life — my friendships, my relationships, and the choices I make for my future.

Things became much worse after they found out about my relationship. I fell in love with someone I met through college, and I wanted the freedom to decide for myself whether this is the person I build a future with. My parents strongly opposed it, and the restrictions and pressure became far more intense.

My phone has been taken away. My movements are questioned constantly. My father has threatened to stop my education. During my exams I was taken out of the state on a trip I didn't choose — I missed exams and now have a backlog to clear. On that trip I was pressured to promise I'd end my relationship and marry someone chosen for me. I did not make that promise freely.

I have very little privacy, and I've been told that choosing independence means being cut off. As long as I depend on them, I don't know how much of my own future I'll get to decide.

I don't want anyone to fight my family for me. I don't want revenge. I just want the ability to make decisions about my own life.

I'm building skills, looking for freelance work, and saving so I can support myself and leave safely. If you can give any amount, it goes straight towards that. If you can't, please share this.

    Questions you might have

    Why is this anonymous?

    Her phone, money and movements are monitored. Being identified would make things worse at home. Anonymity keeps her safe until she's out.

    How do I know this is real?

    You can't verify her like a registered charity, and we won't pretend otherwise. Every contribution and every rupee spent is logged in the updates below.

    Who runs this page?

    A few of her friends from college. Nobody takes a cut.

    Why doesn't she just leave?

    Leaving with no money, no place to stay and no income isn't independence — it's a crisis. She needs a financial cushion first.
